# Q3 Roadmap

In Q3, we will focus on the following integrations and development milestones:

## 1. Music NFTs (on TOKO)

Earlier in June, we had a productive meeting with the team at TOKO/Dragginz—discussing use cases for NFTs within the music industry. Our plan is to collaborate on unique POA (proof-of-attendance) NFTs, rewarding fans who attend live experiences with a digital memento and allowing artists to engage fans and capture attendee data at their live shows. TRAX will leverage its existing network of artists and event venues to pilot the project in Q4.

## 2. TRAX Premium

We will be shifting towards a freemium business model with a tiered product offering. Premium features including new unlock methods, AI visualiser, and direct messaging functionality will be available to all Premium creators. This will support revenue growth and a path to profitability for the project.

## 3. LYRA

Our AI visualiser is now in its final phase of testing. We are working on integration with the TRAX application—enabling artists to use the tool to create unique, dynamic visualisations of their music and share with their fans.

## 4. In-App Notifications

With the imminent release of our iOS app, we want to improve the mobile experience for artists by enabling them to notify fans of new releases via in-app notifications. This provides another route for artists to directly reach their fans and should improve general usage rates of the app.

## 5. Messages

We want to give artists tools to re-contact the audience they build on TRAX. SMS and email sending services will allow an artist to send messages directly to their fanbase.

## 6. Continued UI & UX Improvements

We are continuing to simplify and optimise the UX (particularly for mobile users). Planned improvements include:

- Refined layout
- Improvements to wallet and settings pages
- Media player which maintains content while navigating around the app
- Seamless content upload

# Q2 Development Review

We made some major changes to the TRAX platform in Q2. The overarching goal was to transition away from competing with streaming/media platforms and towards being an easy-to-use tool that empowers artists to share exclusive content while growing their fanbase, audience data, and revenue streams.

## Access Control & Unlock Methods

We expanded the number of ways an artist can release on TRAX. Artists can now create releases with the following unlock methods:

- **Email/Phone Unlock**  
  Users can now unlock exclusive content by submitting either their email or phone number. This helps artists grow their mailing list while giving fans seamless access to premium drops.

- **Hidden Code Unlock**  
  Artists can now share secret codes with fans to unlock hidden content.

> We are continuing to expand the number of unlock methods—focussing on more complex integrations with other platforms (e.g. "Follow on Instagram to unlock", "Pre-save on Spotify to unlock").

---

## Authentication & Fan Onboarding

### Auto-Generated Accounts for Unsigned Users

When fans unlock content without signing up, an account is automatically created in the background. Onboarding has never been easier.

### Phone Number Sign-Up

A quick and convenient way to join the platform — users can now sign up using only their phone number.

### Sign in with OISY

After v3.4.2 (iOS release) is approved, we will release v3.4.3, which will include OISY wallet as a native sign-in and wallet connection.

### Magic Codes for Email Sign-In

We are deprecating passwords to make sign-in more secure and convenient. Passwords will be replaced with code-based access.

## Artist Onboarding & Profile Tools

We have completely refactored artist profiles with complete customisation—allowing artists to control colour, font, font-size, images, and more.

Artists can now use their TRAX profile as a functional link-in-bio tool by adding social links and other buttons.

### New Profile Editor for Artists

- Add custom links and socials  
- Edit the look and feel with custom colours and fonts

### Mailing List Signup on Artist Profiles (Email/SMS)

Fans can now subscribe directly to an artist's mailing list via their profile page—even if they're not signed in. We'll auto-create an account for them, keeping the experience frictionless.

### Studio Page Revamp

- Refreshed look and feel  
- Key content metrics where creators need them most

### New "Become a Creator" Flow

An intuitive 3-step process to start creating.

### All-New Artist Profile Page

A reimagined design putting the spotlight on the artist, their brand, and their content.

### New Upload Flow with Preview Feature

- Faster and more intuitive  
- Artists can now preview content before publishing
